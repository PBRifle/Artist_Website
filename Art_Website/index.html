Commission a piece

Most commissions take four to eight weeks from approved sketch to delivery.

1

You describe the piece

Subject, rough size, and where it will hang. A few reference photos help if it's a portrait or a pet.

2

You receive a quote and sketch

A price, a timeline, and a small preliminary sketch so we agree on composition before paint goes on canvas.

3

The painting begins

Once you approve the sketch, I start. You'll get two or three progress photos along the way.

4

Varnishing and delivery

The finished piece is varnished, framed on request, and shipped in a padded crate or delivered locally.

About

I've been painting in acrylic for over fifteen years, first as a restorer's assistant and then on my own. I'm drawn to ordinary rooms, working harbors, and faces I know well — subjects that reward a slow, layered approach rather than a quick one.

Every commission is painted by hand in the studio, no assistants or shortcuts. I photograph work in progress so you can follow along, and I'm always honest early if something isn't working, rather than late.
